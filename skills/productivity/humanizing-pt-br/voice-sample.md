# Voice sample: repository owner

Use this only when the text being edited is the repository owner's own writing
and no other sample was given. For anyone else, ask for their sample.

## Sample

> O Unified Core Platform é um software de automação de envio de mensagens
> ilimitadamente sem dependência de servidores ou serviços terceiros. O grande
> problema de macros que enviam mensagens automaticamente, é terem suas contas
> do WhatsApp banidas por spam.
>
> O Unified Core Platform consegue digitar as mensagens como um humano faria,
> desta forma as chances de ter a conta banida diminuem drasticamente, além
> disso é possível enviar mensagens diferentes alternadamente, também para
> diminuir a chance de ter a conta banida, além disso, é possível enviar
> mensagens para contatos que não tenham te adicionado ainda, como não é
> possível fazer em listas de transmissão.

## Habits to leave alone

These read as tells in the abstract and are this writer's voice in practice. The
sample overrides the pattern, so do not edit them out.

- **Long sentences chained with commas.** A paragraph can be a single sentence
  carrying three claims. Do not split it into three just to vary rhythm.
- **`além disso` and `desta forma` as connectors, repeated inside one
  paragraph.** This directly overrides P7. The second paragraph uses `além
  disso` twice and that is normal for this writer.
- **`-mente` adverbs, freely.** `ilimitadamente`, `automaticamente`,
  `drasticamente`, `alternadamente` appear in two short paragraphs. Keep them,
  and never convert them into `de forma + adjetivo` (P10).
- **The full subject repeated at the start of a paragraph** instead of a
  pronoun: *O Unified Core Platform* opens both.
- **A comma between a long subject and its verb**, as in *O grande problema de
  macros que enviam mensagens automaticamente, é terem...*. Non-standard, and
  characteristic. Leave it unless the user asked for copy-editing.
- **Flat, unhedged claims.** No `talvez`, no `pode ser que`. Do not add hedging.
- **No bold, no bullet lists, no travessões** in running prose.

## What the sample does not cover

It is product documentation: two paragraphs of technical register. It says
nothing about how this writer handles opinion, humour, or personal narrative.
For a blog post or an essay, ask for a sample of that kind of writing rather
than stretching this one.

Restating information across sentences (*diminuem drasticamente as chances de
ter a conta banida*, then *também para diminuir a chance de ter a conta banida*)
appears here, but it is loose drafting rather than voice. Tightening it is fair.
